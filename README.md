## 👨‍🏫 About
I am a Faculty Lecturer (Assistant Professor) at the **Berlin School of Business & Innovation (BSBI)** in Berlin, Germany. My research focuses on **Computer Vision, Deep Learning and Applied Artificial Intelligence**, with applications in medical imaging, smart farming, robotics, explainable AI, federated learning, human activity recognition, crowd analytics and intelligent industrial systems.

I received my **PhD in Computer Vision & Artificial Intelligence from Multimedia University, Malaysia (2023)** and previously worked as a **Post-Doctoral Scientist at Multimedia University (2023–2025)**. Earlier research appointments included the **University of Ulsan** and **Sungkyunkwan University** in South Korea.

## 🔬 Research Directions
- 👁️ **Computer Vision & Deep Learning** — image/video understanding, detection, recognition and visual intelligence
- 🩺 **Medical AI & Explainable AI** — medical imaging, disease classification, XAI and interpretable decision support
- 🌱 **Smart Farming & Precision Agriculture** — crop disease recognition, lightweight vision models and Agricultural IoT
- 🤖 **Robotics & Intelligent Systems** — robot vision, HRI, autonomous perception and intelligent automation
- 🔐 **Federated & Privacy-Preserving AI** — federated learning, privacy-aware modelling and explainability
- 🏭 **Industrial AI** — fault diagnosis, wafer-defect classification and intelligent condition monitoring
